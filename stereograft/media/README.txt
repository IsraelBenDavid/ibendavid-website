Video files for the StereoGraft page go here, by section:
  teaser/   teaser_anaglyph.mp4, teaser_sbs.mp4 (+ optional .jpg posters)
  t2s/      <id>_anaglyph.mp4, <id>_sbs.mp4   ids: wan21_720p_1..4, wan21_1080p_1..4, ltx23_720p_1..4, ltx23_1080p_1..4
  v2s/      <id>_anaglyph.mp4, <id>_sbs.mp4   ids: wan21_1..4, ltx23_1..4
  compare/  <example>/<method>_d<setting>_anaglyph.mp4, _sbs.mp4 (+ .webp posters), thumb.webp   (ready; listed in ../static/data/compare.json)
  method/   the two clips of the video-to-stereo method figure (ready)
The ids come from the config block at the top of ../index.html. See ../README.md for conventions and ffmpeg recipes.
